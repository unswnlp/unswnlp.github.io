  abstract = {Policymakers in defence and defence-aligned sectors must monitor rapidly evolving research alongside sector priorities relevant to operational and strategic needs. In practice, these sources are fragmented across heterogeneous formats, disjoint repositories, making capability discovery slow and difficult to audit. We present Didact, a prototype that integrates two sources: publicly available defence reports and policy documents from Australia, and a purpose-built knowledge graph derived from Australian research publications. Didact provides natural language conversations for policy-oriented workflows, and devises a composite retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline that allows synthesis of information from both sources. A key feature of Didact is an interactive Evidence Rail that presents retrieved document evidence and, when research-graph retrieval is used, visualises relevant entities and relationships. Automated evaluation on DoRA-QnA50 characterises Didact's retrieval quality, response quality, and runtime. While Didact has been co-developed as an academia-industry project for the Australian defence context, it is adaptable to other domains where knowledge is similarly fragmented and reliability is critical. A demonstration video is available here: https://youtu.be/KRtlq3LQ9WA.},
