<h2 id="intended-use">Intended use<a class="headerlink" href="#intended-use" title="Permanent link">¶</a></h2>
<div class="admonition warning">
<p class="admonition-title">Research software</p>
<p>TriageSim generates <strong>synthetic</strong> dialogue for research on speech and
language systems. It is not a clinical decision support tool, has not been
clinically validated, and must not be used to triage real patients or to
inform real clinical decisions.</p>
<p>Dialogues it produces are generated artefacts, not records of real
encounters. Label them as synthetic in any dataset you release.</p>
</div>
